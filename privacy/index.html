SHARPLINE

Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

The short version: we collect what we need to run SharpLine — your account, your picks and bets, and your settings. We don't sell your data and we don't show ads. You can delete everything from the app at any time.

1. What we collect

  • Account: your email address, username, avatar and sign-in information.
  • Your activity: picks you make, bets you log (team, line, price, units, result), fan picks, favorite teams and sports, and your settings.
  • Game Chat: posts, reactions, reports and blocks.
  • Notifications and Live Activities: your device's push and Live Activity tokens and your notification choices, so we can send the alerts you turned on.
  • Subscription status: whether you have Pro, your plan, and trial and renewal dates. Payment details are handled by Apple — we never see your card.
  • Things you choose to tell us: how you heard about SharpLine, the reason you give if you cancel, and feedback or support emails.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports and error logs (device model, iOS version, app version) so we can fix bugs.

We don't collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or betting-account logins.

2. How we use it

  • to run the app: save your picks and bets, grade them, and show your record;
  • to send the notifications and Live Activities you turned on;
  • to provide Pro and the free trial, including a reminder before a trial ends;
  • to keep Game Chat safe;
  • to improve SharpLine — for example, learning which features people use and why people cancel;
  • to answer support requests and to meet legal obligations.

3. Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising. We share it only with services that help us run SharpLine, and only for that purpose:

  • Supabase — database and sign-in
  • Railway — our server
  • RevenueCat and Apple — subscriptions
  • Expo and Apple — push notifications and app updates
  • Sentry — crash reports

Your username, avatar, Game Chat posts and (if you choose) your record can be seen by other users. Leaderboard and public-record settings are in the app under More → Privacy. We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect SharpLine and its users.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. Backups are kept for up to 14 days. When you delete your account, your data is deleted, and removed from backups as they expire.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: More → Manage account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your picks, bets, record, chat posts and profile.
  • Notifications: turn each one on or off in More → Alerts & Notifications, or in your iPhone's Settings.
  • Access or correct your data: email us and we'll help.

Depending on where you live (for example, California), you may have extra rights to know, access, correct or delete your information. Email us to use them; we won't treat you differently for doing so.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with access controls. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

7. Age

SharpLine is only for people 21 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 21. If you believe someone under 21 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

8. Changes

We'll update the date at the top when this policy changes, and tell you in the app about important changes.
