Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → SharpLine, then tap Cancel Subscription. You keep SharpLine Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I get a refund?

App Store purchases are refunded by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I bought Pro but it isn't unlocked.

Open the subscription screen in SharpLine and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID you bought with.

I'm not getting notifications.

Check More → Notifications in SharpLine to turn on the alerts you want, and make sure notifications are allowed for SharpLine in your iPhone's Settings.

How do I delete my account?

In SharpLine, go to Account → Delete account. This removes your profile, picks, logged bets and chat posts. If you subscribe, also cancel in your Apple ID settings so you aren't billed again.

Can I place bets in SharpLine?

No. SharpLine is for predictions and tracking only, and is for entertainment. You can log bets you place elsewhere to keep your record in one place.

If you or someone you know has a gambling problem, call 1-800-MY-RESET.
